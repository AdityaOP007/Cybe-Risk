# Risk Engine

> **Status**: 🔲 Planned — Not yet implemented

## Purpose

Quantifies cyber risk in financial terms by combining asset criticality, vulnerability severity, threat likelihood, and security control effectiveness.

## Planned Capabilities

- Risk scoring per asset and organizational unit
- Financial impact estimation (Annualized Loss Expectancy)
- Risk aggregation and trending
- Integration with AI/ML engine for predictive scoring

## Dependencies

- Database & Data Models (Module 02)
- Asset Management (Module 03)
- Security Telemetry (Module 04)
- Threat Intelligence (Module 05)
