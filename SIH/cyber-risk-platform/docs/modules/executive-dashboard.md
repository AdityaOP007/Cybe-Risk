# Module 12: Executive Cyber Risk & Decision Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview
The Executive Dashboard serves as the command center for the Cyber Risk Quantification Platform. It is designed for non-technical executives (CISO, CFO, Board) to understand the organization's security posture, financial exposure, compliance status, and required decisions at a glance.

## Architecture
The dashboard acts as an aggregation layer over Modules 6 through 11. 
It intentionally **does not** duplicate any business logic, ML inference, or risk calculation. Instead, the `DashboardAggregatorService` queries the latest persisted results from the respective domain modules to construct a unified `ExecutiveDashboardData` payload.

### Backend Components
- **`DashboardAggregatorService`**: Located in `app/services/dashboard/aggregator.py`. Pulls risk scores, financial metrics, forecasts, threats, vulnerabilities, recommendations, budgets, and compliance coverage into a single Pydantic model.
- **Dynamic Alerts & Insights**: The service dynamically generates executive-level insights and tracks stateful alerts (`DashboardAlert`) using a fingerprinting system to avoid duplicates.

### Frontend Components
The UI is broken down into modular, responsive React components located in `src/components/dashboard`:
- **`ExecutiveHeader`**: Displays data freshness and model health.
- **`TopKpiCards`**: The critical top-level metrics (Risk Score, Financial Exposure, Forecast, Compliance).
- **`TopRiskAssets`**: Identifies where the most risk is concentrated.
- **`ActionQueue`**: A prioritized list of required executive decisions (e.g., approving a budget or mitigating a critical vulnerability).
- **`BudgetImpactChart`**: Visualizes the residual risk if a proposed cybersecurity portfolio is funded.
- **`ComplianceSummaryCards`**: Summarizes the control coverage across frameworks like NIST, ISO, RBI, and SEBI.

## Data Freshness & Trust
Executives must trust the data they are seeing. 
1. **Model Health**: The header displays a green/red indicator for the health of underlying engines (Risk, Financial, Prediction, Compliance).
2. **Last Updated**: Timestamps are prominently displayed to indicate when the underlying models last ran.
3. **No Fake AI**: We explicitly label model outputs (e.g., "Modeled Financial Exposure", "30-Day Risk Forecast") instead of using buzzwords.

## KPI Definitions
- **Overall Cyber Risk**: The organizational risk score (0-100) calculated by Module 6.
- **Modeled Financial Exposure**: The potential financial loss (Value at Risk) calculated by the FAIR model in Module 7.
- **30-Day Risk Forecast**: The predicted risk score 30 days from now, generated by the LSTM/RandomForest ensemble in Module 8.
- **Average Compliance Coverage**: The average percentage of implemented, evidence-backed controls across all tracked frameworks (Module 11).
- **Priority Exposure**: Assets with the highest combination of inherent criticality and current risk score.
